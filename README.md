# CENTRIFUGAL

Orbital mechanics for everybody, in 16-bit pixels. Launch rockets from
procedural planets, plan your burns on the predicted path, visit the
moon, the neighbors and the gas giant, and land your booster back on the
pad. Play it at https://centrifugal.helico.app (keyboard + mouse).

`cargo run --release` to play locally. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
